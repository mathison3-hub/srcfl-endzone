Formula: 35% this-season projected points · 25% last-season total points · 40% value discount (keeper cost round vs. current ADP round — the bigger the gap, the bigger the steal). Scored 0–100 per player, team score is the average of both keepers.
Rule: a player can be kept a maximum of 2 consecutive years — after that, he goes back into the draft pool regardless of score. First-round picks can never be kept.
